# Sales Performance Dashboard (2016–2020)

## Project Overview

An interactive sales dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance from 2016 to 2020. The dashboard summarizes key business metrics and enables users to explore sales and profit performance across product categories, cities, and account managers.

## Tools & Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive Slicers
- KPI Reporting
- Exploratory Data Analysis

## Key Metrics

- **Total Sales:** Rp16.51 billion
- **Total Orders:** 1,000
- **Profit Margin:** 43.43%
- **Total Profit:** Rp7.17 billion

## Dashboard Preview

![Sales Dashboard](images/dashboard-preview.png)

## Dashboard Features

- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Sales and profit comparison by product category
- Sales performance by city
- Order distribution by product category and city
- Top 5 account managers ranked by profit
- Interactive filters for city, product category, and year

## Insights

- Technology recorded the highest total profit among the three product categories.
- Surabaya recorded the highest total sales among the three cities shown in the dashboard.
- Monthly sales fluctuated throughout the observation period, with several noticeable peaks.

## Recommendations

- Examine product-level sales and profit margins to identify which Technology products contribute most to profitability.
- Investigate sales volume and average order value across cities to understand differences in sales performance.
- Review monthly sales patterns to identify potential seasonality and support inventory planning.
